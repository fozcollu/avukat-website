Under Turkish law, the division of property acquired during marriage is largely determined by the **matrimonial property regime** between the spouses. Unless otherwise agreed, the **participation in acquired property regime** applies from the date of marriage.

## Acquired Property vs. Personal Property

The first step in a property division case is determining whether an asset is "acquired property" or "personal property." As a general rule, income earned through work during the marriage, and movable or immovable property purchased with such income, is considered acquired property. Property obtained through inheritance or gratuitous acquisition is considered personal property.

## Contribution Claims

If one spouse has contributed to the acquisition, preservation, or increase in value of the other spouse's personal property, a claim proportional to that contribution may be raised during divorce. Calculating this contribution claim requires a case-specific technical assessment.

## How Does the Process Work?

A property division case can be filed independently of the divorce case, even after the divorce decree becomes final. However, handling both processes together often saves our clients time and cost.
